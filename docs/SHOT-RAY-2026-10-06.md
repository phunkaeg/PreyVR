# Shots from the muzzle (`aim.shot`) and held triggers (`move.firehold`) — 2026-10-06

Two defects in how a held weapon fires, both measured and fixed on the headless OpenXR mock
(Prey Steam build, `PreyDll.dll` 7d6e322f…), **not yet in a headset**:

1. **The shot converged from the eye.** The aim lane writes the controller's direction into the
   cached reticle ray but keeps its origin at the eye (`aim.origin 0`). Every firearm asks that
   ray where to aim and then fires from its own muzzle toward the answer, so the projectile
   leaves the barrel at an angle and lands where a line *through the eye*, parallel to the
   controller, meets the scene. Measured with the GLOO from the hip: **19.4°** off the barrel at
   1.3 m, **14.9°** at 2 m, 3.7° at 2.5 m with the hand in front. With `aim.shot 1`:
   **≤ 0.004°** on every route, every distance.
2. **A held trigger was a single press.** The fire lane posted the trigger button's press and
   release, but never the per-frame *held* event the engine generates for a physical key, so
   every "while held" weapon action was dead: the GLOO fired once per pull (2 s held → 1 shot;
   now 17) and the Disruptor could not charge. With `move.firehold 1` (default) both work.

Off-by-default switches, as agreed for changes in the aim lane: `aim.shot` (command, VR Options
row, launcher flag). `move.firehold` is on by default because without it two weapons are broken.

## The native contract (static, from the installed binary)

| What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `CArkWeapon::GetReticleInfoForFiring` (R-014) | `0x1694890` | Reads the cached ray **at call time** through the getter, raycasts `origin + dir * weapon+0x344`, returns `{IEntity*, Vec3 target}`. |
| `IArkPlayer::GetReticleViewPositionAndDir` (R-013) | `0x157CBB0` | The getter (player+0x40 vtable[0]); copies `+0x17D4..+0x17EB`. |
| `CArkWeapon::GetFiringPosition` | `0x1694BC0` | Ammo helper (`weapon+0x2F0`) world position via `0x11A5CF0`; falls back to the **camera** (`byte +0 = 1`) if something is within `weapon+0x3F0(+0x3F4)` in front of the owner, or between camera and muzzle. |
| Helper world TM | `0x11A5CF0` | `(Matrix34* out, IEntity*, slot, const char* name, bool 1, bool 0)`; called exactly like `GetFiringPosition` does (entity `weapon+0x40`, slot 0). |
| `CArkWeapon::GetReticlePosition` (R-015) | `0x1694A20` | `origin + dir * range` of the cached ray (weapon vtable `+0x148`). |
| Projectile spawner | `0x1677040` | `(launcher, &position, &direction, …)` — 10 arguments. |
| Shotgun-family pellet spawn | `0x16AAB70` | `(weapon, &firingPos, &muzzleRot, &target, …)`; centres its pattern on `0x16A7050`, which returns the target only for aim assist / a direct hit and otherwise **`GetReticlePosition`**. |

The four callers of the firing query, by return address, and what they do with it:

| Route | Return RVA | Weapons seen | Projectile direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| GLOO `OnPreRender` `0x169F420` | `0x169F492` | GLOO | `normalize(target − firingPos)` (firing position asked **before** the query, flags `0x8000`) |
| Shotgun-family `StartAttack` `0x16AAFF0` | `0x16AB0C3` | pistol, crossbow (toy gun), shotgun | pellets toward `0x16A7050`'s point (firing position asked **after**, flags 0) |
| `0x13BB530` | `0x13BB791` | **Disruptor** (identified live) | arc from the firing position to the target |
| `0x16A29E0` | `0x16A2CC8` | not seen in this save | conditional query; origin = the ammo helper |

## The fix (`src/dll/ShotRay.cpp`, pure parts in `include/preyvr/ShotRay.h`)

The identity it relies on: if the query's ray starts at the very point the projectile spawns
from, `target − spawn` **is** the aim direction. Nothing about the shot is computed by the mod —
range, collision mask, entity hits, spread and point-blank handling stay the engine's.

1. **Shot time** — `GetReticleInfoForFiring` is hooked. For the local player's equipped weapon,
   with a usable aim sample, it asks `GetFiringPosition` (through its hooked entry, with that
   route's flags) where the projectile will spawn, puts that point in the cached origin, calls
   the original and restores the eye. The direction already is the aim lane's (controller, or
   the two-hand solver's).
2. **Shot scope** — the pistol/shotgun/crossbow pellet function reads the cached ray a second
   time (`GetReticlePosition`). Between the steered query and that function's return, on that
   thread only, the getter hook returns the firing position as origin. Without this the
   crossbow's dart still converged from the eye: 1.22° at 2.1 m (deterministic, 7/7 shots),
   0.35–1.33° across poses; with it, **0.000°**.
3. **Spawn** — when `GetFiringPosition` falls back to the camera, a VR test asks the physical
   question instead of the flat-screen one: eye → hand and hand → muzzle both clear (scene
   queries) put the shot back at the muzzle; either blocked keeps the engine's fallback, which is
   what stops shots through walls. (`shot::DecideSpawn`, tested.) Not triggered in 70+ test
   shots: Prey lowers the weapon near walls before the fallback matters.
4. **Per frame** — the published aim sample starts at the muzzle (`sample.muzzleOrigin`), so the
   scene query, the HUD and scene reticles and the debug overlay all describe the shot's ray.
   **The engine's cached ray is never left edited**: interaction, target selection and the
   wrench keep the eye-origin ray they were written for.

Every hook is prologue-checked and fails closed; `aim.shot 0` installs them as passive
observers so the old behaviour can be measured in the same session.

### The held trigger (`src/dll/MoveLane.cpp`)

CryEngine turns a key that stays down into one `eIS_Down` event per frame from its list of held
symbols; a posted press carries no device symbol and never joins that list. The fire lane now
posts `kStateDown` for the trigger button on every frame between its press and release
(`fireHoldPosted` in `report`). `move.firehold 0` restores press/release only for A/B.

- The muzzle the shot leaves from is the **drawn** muzzle: the amber ray leaves the GLOO's nozzle
  in the image (`evidence/shot-ray-2026-10-06/gloo_ray_leaves_nozzle.png`). Its helper axis is
  parallel to the controller's aim (`barrelAxisMdeg=0` for GLOO, pistol and crossbow; the
  Disruptor's `muzzle` helper is authored rotated 90°, as recorded in RE-VR-INTERFACE).
- The muzzles sit off the controller's aim line by 9 mm (crossbow), 65 mm (pistol, Disruptor)
  and 113 mm (GLOO, the nozzle is above the grip) — parallel offsets, like a real gun's bore
  over its grip. The shot follows the barrel, so it lands that far from where the bare
  controller ray would, at every distance; the drawn ray starts at the muzzle and shows it.
- Pistol shot at a GLOO mass on the amber marker breaks it
  (`evidence/shot-ray-2026-10-06/pistol_breaks_gloo.png`); Disruptor arc leaves the prongs along
  the barrel (`disruptor_arc_mirror.png`).

## Debug overlay additions

- With `aim.shot 1` the right ray starts at the muzzle (label `MUZZLE`) and ends on the shot's
  scene hit; the dashed raw controller ray stays for comparison.
- **The last shot** is drawn in green for 4 s: from where the projectile actually left (read
  from the spawn functions' arguments) to the target, with its angle against the aim; the status
  panel adds `Shot <route> <deg> spawn <kind> <mm> off aim`. `dbg.marks` lists it.
- Gameplay rays and the shot trace are hidden while the HUD does not own gameplay input (menus,
  death screen); they had been drawn across the death menu.

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `aim.shot [0\|1]` | Bare: report (and install the observers). 1 = shots from the muzzle, 0 = native. |
| `aim.shotprobe [1\|0]` | Research: records who reads the cached ray (return RVAs and counts) while on. |
| `move.firehold [0\|1]` | Held-trigger events (default 1). |
| VR Options → Setup → **Shots from the muzzle (experimental)** | Persisted as `muzzle_aim`; a saved "off" never overrides a launcher/command "on". |
| `Start-PreyVR.ps1 -MuzzleAim` | Sends `aim.shot 1` before `vr.enable`. |

Per shot, `PreyVR.log` gets `preyvr_shot n=… route=… steered=… spawnKind=… spawn=… dir=… target=…
angleDeg=… spawnOffAimMm=… spawnFromQueryMm=…` and, when a spawn function saw the projectile,
`projDir upDeg sideDeg`.

## Found, not fixed: the Disruptor's charge glow is drawn at the wrong depth

At full charge two effects appear: a flare at the prong tips (correct in both eyes) and a pale
cyan glow with a spark over the coil. **The glow has about 105 px more crossed disparity than
the coil it belongs to** (left eye: right of the coil; right eye: left of it), which puts it
roughly **20 cm from the eyes while the coil is ~50 cm away**: in the headset it floats in front
of the weapon, toward the face. The offset is the same with the near-pass stereo edit on and off
(`near.enable 0/1`: 102 / 105 px), so the glow is drawn with the weapon's near pass and its error
does not come from `NearViewStereo`'s offset. Evidence: `disruptor_coil_glow_stereo.png`,
`disruptor_coil_glow_near_ab.png`. Next step: identify the effect (an emitter-creation trace like
Dishonored VR's `gunfxlog`) and how its position reaches the near pass. Holding at full charge for
a few seconds discharges by itself (native).

  Full CTest 61/61.
