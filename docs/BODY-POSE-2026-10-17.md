# The visible body: a full body that stands, leans, squats and steps under the player

Branch `jordi/body-torso` (on `jordi/stereo-dual-test`). Measured on the mock (simulated runtime, Steam
`PreyDll.dll`), not yet in a headset. It builds on `BODY-FOLLOW-2026-10-16.md`: the character already turns with
the head (the engine culls against the character's aim) and walks with the player around the room. That left the
visible body turning 1:1 with the head, standing up through any crouch, sliding its feet, and floating its torso
away from a lean. This part makes the first-person body behave like the ones in the full-body VR games and mods.

Evidence: [`evidence/body-pose-2026-10-17/report.txt`](evidence/body-pose-2026-10-17/report.txt) (sheets on disk).

## The precedent it follows

We followed the precedents below. FRIK, Doom 3 BFG VR, Quake VR, REFramework, UEVR, Valheim VR and FinalIK were
read from source; VRIK from its changelog and FAQ; Lone Echo from the GDC 2017 slides with notes.

- **Torso yaw:** Quake VR 1.0.1's `vr_torso.cpp`, after Lone Echo's chest estimate. It is a weighted vote of
  the head now (1), the head's last 0.3 s (1), both hands' midpoint while they are held in front (2.5), one hand
  alone (0.3), and hands hanging at the sides (2, square to the line between them). It turns only past
  8 degrees, then eases at 12/s, and is never more than 70 degrees from the head.
  - Quake counts hands 5-25 cm in front of the shoulders; Lone Echo counts reaching hands too. We count up to
    60 cm, because a pistol held at the chest is ~40 cm out.
  - Inside the deadzone it settles at 1/s, so a body at rest ends up square to the vote.
  - The hands are measured from where the body stands, not from the head, so a lean that carries the head over
    them is not read as a turn.
  - While the stick walks head-relative, the torso faces the view.
- **Lean:** a lean radius of 25 cm (Quake's `vr_lean_radius`).
  - Within it the body does not jump to the head. It comes back under it at the head's own speed (at least
    0.5 m/s), except while the player is leaning. Leaning means the neck is 2-15 cm lower than standing and the
    crown is tilted the way the neck is going (Quake: "head lower and tilted toward it").
  - The spine bends to reach the head, most of it at the hips (FinalIK's VRIK).
- **Crouch:** physical crouch triggers the game's crouch, with hysteresis: down at 0.30 m of neck drop (Meta's
  avatar legs), up at 0.20 m. The hips drop and sit back as a squat's do (FinalIK `moveBodyBackWhenCrouching`),
  and the knees fold.
- **Feet:** feet stay planted while the body sways over them. They step one at a time, in a 7 cm arc, once one
  has drifted 20 cm from where the body would stand it or the leg could no longer reach it (FinalIK
  `stepThreshold`, Quake's planted feet).
- **Head in walls:** fade to black stays the default; the option allows seeing through.

## How it works

### The body pose: one rigid move per joint group (AnimIkTakeover)

- **Joint groups.** Every joint of the 101-joint first-person rig is grouped by its nearest named ancestor: the
  hips, each of the four spine segments, the neck and head, and each thigh, shin and foot. `bodypose::Solve`
  (`include/preyvr/BodyPose.h`, pure, unit-tested) gives each group one rigid move:
  - the torso turned to the torso estimate, about the vertical through the head's pivot;
  - the spine bent (shares 40/25/20/15 % down from spine1; limits 46 degrees forward, 15 back, 26 sideways) so
    the head's pivot lands over the player's;
  - the hips dropped or raised to the player's height, sitting back by 0.35 × the drop;
  - each leg solved with two bones onto its planted foot, with the knee in the animation's own bend plane.
- **The goal.** It is the player's head-pivot **displacement** from the calibrated rest point, added to the
  animated head pivot. Prey hangs its first-person model ~12 cm below and behind the eye on purpose, and that
  offset stays.
- **When it runs.** The plan is computed before the native ADIK pass, because PrepareArms must start from the
  clavicles the body moved. It is written after the pass, into the absolute pose the engine skins. The arms are
  then solved as before. Each driven hand is turned back by the inverse of its group's rotation, so it keeps
  the rotation the pass gave it on the controller.
- **The camera joints stay put.** ArkPlayerCamera derives the view from the animated `camera_jnt`. With it
  moved, a nod moved the camera 40 mm and every case drifted the play yaw by 0.05-0.08 degrees. So
  `camera_jnt`, `look_jnt` and `hud_jnt` keep the animation's transform. They are not drawn.

### The body follow (BodyFollow)

- **The lean radius.** It replaces the 15 mm start: `body::LeanStep`, `body::Leaning`. A first version only
  walked the part of the offset past the radius. That left the body 25 cm behind after any jump of the head;
  the unit test now covers it.
- **The physical crouch.**
  - It taps the crouch button (`MoveLane RequestCrouchTap`), the path the old Chairloader mod verified;
    `TrySetStance` does nothing. It is guarded by `ArkPlayer::m_stance` (+0x1838; `GetStance`, Steam 0x157CE70),
    and it retries standing every 0.5 s while something overhead stops it.
  - While the crouch is ours, the view is composed about the **standing** eye height: `CrouchEyeLift` raises
    the reference by `standing - game eye`. So the camera stays at the real head and only the body crouches.
  - It never touches the room reference. An earlier version absorbed the eye drop into it and broke on the
    first snap turn, which bumps the reference generation.
  - It releases only when the game's eye is back at standing height. The stance flips a frame before the eye
    starts rising.
- **The vote's body point.** The torso vote uses the room origin as the body's position.

### Movement

MOVEMENT DIRECTION = BODY RELATIVE now walks the way the torso faces (`TorsoYawNow` → MoveLane). Before this, it
was identical to head relative, because the body followed the head.

## Options

On the VIEW tab:
- **PHYSICAL CROUCH:** on/off (`body.crouch`).
- **HEAD IN WALLS:** DARKEN / SEE THROUGH (`body.fadeon`).

Commands: `body.pose 0|1` (with its report), `body.feet 0|1`, `body.lean <radiusMm> <recentreMmps>`,
`body.crouch [0|1] [downMm upMm]`, `arm.torso <deadzoneDeg> <rate‰> <maxDeg>`, and `view.spectate <mm> <azDeg>
<upMm> <atMm>`. The last is a camera outside the player, for looking at the body. It is research only: the
render contract and the hands' anchor (position and yaw) take it back out.

## Measured (mock)

| what | result |
|---|---|
| world anchor, every player-only case (spin, sweep, shake, nod, steps, rock, circle, walls) | 0.22 mm, play yaw 0.000 deg |
| body under the head after steps forward / right / back | 0.598 of 0.6 m, 0.499 of 0.5, 0.5 of 0.5 |
| physical crouch 45 cm down and back up | game crouches and stands (one tap each); camera vertical change 0.0 mm |
| squat 20 cm | hips −21 cm, knees forward, feet planted, bend 12 deg |
| lean forward 22 cm | body held, bend 37 deg at the hips, legs straight |
| over the shoulder 80 deg, hands forward | torso 54 deg behind the head |
| body relative walk, head at 60 deg | travel 16 deg (torso 21 deg) |
| drawn hand vs controller, pose on / off | 18/18 (spin), 32/40 (sweep), 73/84 (shake) mm: the pose adds none |

The hand figures are larger than in the 2026-10-16 note (9-30 mm) with or without the pose. This build renders
both eyes per frame (PR #5), which doubles the frame time the IK lags by.

## Not done / open

- **The shadow.** It comes from the second character instance, which is not edited, so it shows the game's
  own pose.
- **Stick walking.** It plays the game's walk animation (the feet are free then); the torso faces the view.
- **BodyFollow's floor rule** (unchanged) refuses to walk the body onto the edge of a mat or a lying body
  (`noGround`), and once took a 10.8 cm "step" for a frame during a snap turn next to furniture.
- **The mock harness.** It now runs with the desktop locked: `PREYVR_FAKE_FOCUS=1` hooks
  GetForegroundWindow/GetActiveWindow/GetFocus to Prey's own window, `g_pauseOnLoseFocus` /
  `g_hasWindowFocus` are allowlisted, and `menu 4` accepts the loading notice. Scripts are in
  `build/jordi-mock`: BootLocked, BodyViews, Battery, CrouchProbe, Motion and BodyRel.
